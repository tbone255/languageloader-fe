# MVP Language Learning Plan (Reduced Scope)

## Supported Exercise Types (Global)

- Fill in the blank (single word)
- Build a sentence from a word bank (click in order)
- Match image to sentence

---

# LESSON 1 Deixis + Singular Nouns

## Target language

- This / That
- Singular concrete nouns
- “This is X / That is X”

## User outcome

- Identify basic noun phrases
- Associate objects with language and distance

## Lesson 1 Content Requirements

- 6 to 8 concrete nouns
- 2 demonstratives
- 1 core sentence frame

## Lesson 1 Exercises (MVP)

- Sentence → Image Match
- Image → Sentence (Word Bank)
- Gap Fill (single word)

## Lesson 1 SRS Outputs

Minimum: **10 cards**

### Vocab cards (required)

- Front: target noun
- Back: translation + pronunciation
- Count: 6 to 8

### Phrase cloze cards (required)

- Front: “___ is X”
- Back: full sentence + meaning
- Count: 2 to 4

---

# LESSON 2 Pluralization + These / Those

## Target language

- Plural nouns
- These / Those
- Singular vs plural contrast

## User outcome

- Understand plurality
- Distinguish near vs far plural

## Lesson 2 Content Requirements

- Same nouns as Lesson 1, pluralized
- Singular and plural contrast pairs
- Minimal or no new vocabulary

## Lesson 2 Exercises (MVP)

- Sentence → Image Match (plural scenes)
- Image → Sentence (Word Bank)
- Gap Fill (single word)

## Lesson 2 SRS Outputs

Minimum: **10 cards**

### Vocab cards (required)

- Front: plural noun form
- Back: translation + pronunciation
- Count: 6 to 8

### Plural sentence cloze cards (required)

- Front: “___ are X”
- Back: filled sentence + meaning
- Count: 2 to 4

---

# LESSON 3 Possession + “To Be”

## Target language

- my / your
- it is / they are
- Possessive noun phrases

## User outcome

- Express ownership
- Form complete declarative sentences

## Lesson 3 Content Requirements

- Possessives: my, your
- Verb: to be (present tense)
- Sentence frames
  - “This is my X”
  - “They are your X”

## Lesson 3 Exercises (MVP)

- Sentence → Image Match
- Image → Sentence (Word Bank)
- Gap Fill (single word)

## Lesson 3 SRS Outputs

Minimum: **10 cards**

### Possessive cloze cards (required)

- Front: “This is ___ X”
- Back: filled sentence + meaning
- Count: 4 to 6

### Verb agreement cloze cards (required)

- Front: “They ___ X”
- Back: filled sentence + meaning
- Count: 2 to 4

---

## MVP Design Principles

- Only three exercise mechanics
- Reusable UI components across lessons
- Language agnostic structure
- Automatic SRS generation from lesson data
