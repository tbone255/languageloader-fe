# LanguageLoader: Full 100-Lesson Curriculum

*Pashto (pus-001 series). Covers A1 through B1–B2 bridge.*
*See PRODUCT_PLAN.md §5 for pedagogical design principles and phase specs.*

---

## Curriculum Structure

The 100 lessons are organized into **10 units** of approximately 10 lessons each.

Every unit:
- Ends with a roleplay, mini-project, or consolidation activity
- Contains 0–1 embedded **grammar focus inserts** (.5 lessons) that appear at the point of first need
- Builds vocabulary and grammar **simultaneously** — no grammar-first gate

**Grammar inserts** (the .5 lessons) are not standalone grammar lessons. They are short pattern-highlighting sequences that appear at the moment a grammar form first becomes necessary in the thematic content. They should render as 2–3 exercise slot within the preceding lesson's flow, not as a separate lesson card in the path.

## Curriculum Patterns

### Grammar Inserts (.5 Lessons)

Six grammar focus inserts appear at the moment of first need within thematic units:

| Insert | Location | Grammar Introduced |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| 19.5 | Unit 2 | Imperatives (commands, instructions) |
| 26.5 | Unit 3 | Prepositions (in/on/at/under) |
| 35.5 | Unit 4 | Comparative adjectives |
| 52.5 | Unit 6 | Manner adverbs |
| 82.5 | Unit 9 | Discourse connectors (and/but/so/because/when) |
| 97.5 | Unit 10 | Relative clauses (who/which/that) |

**Implementation note:** Grammar inserts are standalone lessons in the path, shorter than a standard lesson (6–10 exercises vs 12–18). They appear as their own node between the surrounding thematic lessons. Their SRS output is `chunk_recall` and `pattern_prompt` cards targeting the introduced pattern.

### Unit-End Exercises

Each unit closes with a higher-stakes synthesis exercise:

| Type | Lessons |
|------|---------|
| Roleplay / `interactive_dialogue` | 40, 70, 90 |
| Mini-project / `personalization_prompt` | 50, 60, 80, 95, 96, 100 |
| Paragraph production | 24, 30, 63, 93 |
| Reading + retell (`story_comprehension`) | 69, 94 |

These are the primary output exercises and generate the highest-value SRS cards (since they require production in full context).

|-------|---------|------------------------|

As the lesson number increases, the SRS card type mix shifts (per PRODUCT_PLAN.md §3.3):

| Phase | Dominant Card Types |
|-------|---------------------|
| L1–20 | `flip_forward`, `audio_to_text` |
| L21–60 | + `chunk_recall`, `flip_reverse` |
| L61–90 | + `pattern_prompt`, extended `chunk_recall` |
| L91–100+ | All types; more `pattern_prompt` and sentence-level production |

## Content Generation Notes (Backend Pipeline)

The backend pipeline (`languageloader-be`) must apply these curriculum rules:

1. **Frequency first:** Each lesson's vocabulary must be drawn from the highest available frequency tier before adding lower-frequency items
2. **Grammar simultaneity:** Grammar patterns must be present in sentence frames from Lesson 1 — never vocabulary lists without grammar context
3. **Grammar inserts:** .5 lesson content is generated as a brief interstitial sequence (2–4 exercises) attached to the preceding lesson's JSON, not as a standalone lesson file
4. **Unit-end exercises:** Roleplay and mini-project lessons require a different generation template — they synthesize across a full unit's vocabulary rather than introducing new items
5. **i+1 calibration:** Each lesson's sentences should use ~85% previously introduced vocabulary; new items are the "+1" and must appear in image-supported context
6. **SRS card graduation:** The pipeline should tag each generated card with `phase` and `difficulty_tier` so the frontend can apply the correct mix
